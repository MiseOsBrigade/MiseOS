# Shimeji Brigade brand guide

Shimeji Brigade is the mascot layer of MiseOS: chibi chef-agents who turn repository work into a visible kitchen service. The tone is warm, capable, playful, and proof-first.

## Visual direction

- **Silhouette:** compact chibi bodies, readable hats, expressive eyes, simple limbs.
- **Motion:** purposeful hops, climbs, perches, and gentle falls; never frantic chaos.
- **Material:** dark kitchen-green environments with gold/orange service lights and teal system accents.
- **Agent distinction:** use hair, hat, apron, badge, or tool silhouette before adding detail.

## Color roles

Use `#07130f` for the stage, `#0f2f25` for panels, `#f2c94c` for primary action, `#e97b22` for heat/attention, `#1e9bb8` for links, `#38a169` for verified state, `#ff6262` for blocked/risk, `#a78bfa` for agent identity, and `#f6f1e7` for readable text.

## Logo rules

Give the mark breathing room equal to the height of the chef hat. Do not stretch, bevel, add gradients outside the supplied mark, or place it on a busy image. The wordmark may be set in all caps with the mark to its left.

## Voice

Say what the brigade is doing, why it matters, and what proof is available. Prefer “Kurogami is checking the pass” over “agent execution initiated.” Never imply autonomous authority beyond the displayed permission and validation state.
