# MiseOS Launch Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Brand foundation
Primary logo, horizontal logo, app icon, favicon, palette, typography, and usage rules.

## Phase 1 — Kurogami
Create front, three-quarter, side, and back views; neutral, happy, thinking, directing, and victory expressions; standing, conducting, reviewing code, deploying, and Brigade-leading poses. Store approved assets under `assets/mascots/kurogami/`.

## Phase 2 — Executive Chefs
Build the ten founding characters in `data/executive-chefs.json` before expanding the remaining roster.

## Phase 3 — Website
Homepage, Platform page, and filterable Brigade gallery. Canonical hero:

> Build Software Like A World-Class Kitchen  
> 75 Specialized Agents. One Operating System.  
> Cook. Orchestrate. Deploy. Observe.

## Phase 4 — Documentation
Publish `docs.miseos.dev` with Getting Started, Agents, Brigade, MCP, Deployments, Vault, Observatory, and SDK sections.

## Phase 5 — DevNFT framework
Begin with Executive Chefs — Series 001 (10 cards), followed by Kitchen Operations and Service Layer.

## Phase 6 — Progressive Brigade expansion
10 Executive, 10 Operations, 10 Service, 10 Engineering, 10 Security, 15 Infrastructure, and 10 Ecosystem agents = 75 launch agents.

## Seven-day launch sprint

| Day | Outcome |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Finalize logo system and Kurogami |
| 2 | Executive Chef collection |
| 3 | Homepage and Platform page |
| 4 | Brigade gallery |
| 5 | Documentation portal |
| 6 | Social launch assets |
| 7 | Public launch |
